# v0.17.0: Stock checked when you order and at pickup

**Two stock checks per order.** Every in-cart item in the order is checked for stock at your store when the order is placed (confirmation email) and again when it's picked up or delivered (receipt email). Both checks are quiet: there's no notification and nothing changes in the cart.

**At pickup or delivery:**
- Items that were in stock both times are marked received automatically.
- You're asked only about items that were out of stock **at either check**: *"Large Eggs was out of stock when you ordered or at pickup. Did it come?"*
- An item neither check could read (for example one that dropped out of Kroger's catalog) is asked about too, worded "out of stock (or couldn't be checked)".
- The buttons and what happens to missing items are unchanged.

**Fallback cases:**
- If the confirmation email was missed, the pickup check alone decides.
- Only if no check could read any item (for example Kroger was unreachable both times) are you asked about everything.

**Fix:** the pickup message now says "at pickup" (or "at delivery").
