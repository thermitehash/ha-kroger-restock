# v0.14.0: Pick the right product from the scanner card

**Fix now opens a pop-up** (it used to jump to the to-do list). For an item that needs review or wasn't found, **Pick the right product** shows up to 5 closest matches from your store, each with size, price, aisle, picture and stock. Tap one to resolve the item: it's added to your Kroger cart if it's in stock, and a barcode fix is remembered for the next scan.

- Not the right ones? Search by name in the same pop-up.
- **Remove item** is there too; it asks before removing.
- On a phone the pop-up opens as a sheet from the bottom.

**Closer matches.** Possible matches are now ranked closest-first (most of your words in the brand and name). Ties keep Kroger's own order.

**New action `kroger_restock.search_products`** (`query`, optional `limit` 1-10): searches your store's catalog, closest first, and returns product ID, name, brand, size, price, aisle, picture and stock. It changes nothing.

**Recently scanned sensor:** items that need review now carry their `candidates` (up to 5).

Items that already needed review before this update keep their old matches, which have no price, aisle or picture. Search in the pop-up to get the full details.
