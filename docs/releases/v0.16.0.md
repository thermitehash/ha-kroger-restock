# v0.16.0: Stock check when you order; only asked about what sold out

**When your order is placed** (the confirmation email arrives through order tracking), every in-cart item in that order is checked for stock at your store. This happens quietly: there's no notification and nothing changes in the cart. An item that has sold out shows **In cart · out of stock now** on the list and in the scanner card.

**When the order is picked up or delivered** (receipt email):
- The items that were in stock are marked received automatically.
- You're asked only about the ones that were out of stock: *"Large Eggs was out of stock when you ordered. Did it come?"*
  - One item: **Yes, it came** / **No**.
  - Several: **All came** / **None came** / **Some missing…** (type which didn't come).
- Anything that didn't come goes back on the list, then back into the cart for your next order once it's in stock.
- If nothing was out of stock, you just get *"N items marked received."*, with no question.
- Items added to the cart after the order was placed aren't touched.

If the stock check didn't run (for example the confirmation email was missed), the pickup notification asks about everything, as before.

With **Notify on order updates** off, in-stock items are still marked received; out-of-stock ones stay in the cart for you to check off.
