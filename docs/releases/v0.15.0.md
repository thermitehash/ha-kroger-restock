# v0.15.0: Fix button with a count

**New Fix button on the scanner card**, next to Scan:
- It shows how many items need a product picked (needs review or not found), for example **Fix 3**. It counts every such item on the list, not only recent ones.
- Tap it to go through them one by one in the pick pop-up, which shows *1 of 3*. Pick a match, **Skip** to leave one for later, or **Done** to stop.
- It's greyed out with no number when nothing needs fixing.

The per-row **Fix** buttons still open the pop-up for that one item.

**Recently scanned sensor:** new `needs_fix` (every item that needs a product picked, oldest first, with its matches) and `needs_fix_count`. Both are kept out of history.
