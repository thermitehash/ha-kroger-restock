# v0.17.2: Product lookup and "received" events for other apps

**New action `kroger_restock.lookup_product`** (response only): send a barcode (`upc`) or Kroger `product_id` and get back the product's name, brand, size, aisle, price and stock, plus every picture (best front picture first) and the largest front picture as `image_url`. It returns the printed `barcode` too.
- It's read-only: no list item, no cart push, no events.
- It uses the same barcode matching as scans, and results are cached for 7 days.
- PLU codes, unknown codes and errors such as rate limits return `found: false` with a `reason`. It never falls back to a fuzzy name search.

**New event `kroger_restock_received`:** fires once per item when it's marked received, whether from the order-complete notification, the automatic marking at pickup, `mark_cart_received`, or checking it off in the to-do list.
- It carries the item, its barcode, name, brand, size, picture, the quantity received, the price and the order number.
- The same units are never reported twice, and items reported missing don't fire.

**New action `kroger_restock.recent_received`** (response only): the last 14 days of received items, for apps that were offline when the events fired.
